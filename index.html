Remindr!

Alarms, timers, stopwatch, world clock, to-do tasks, calendar, notes, currency & calculator — all in one app.

Key Features

Alarms, Timers & Stopwatch

Full-screen alarms with custom ringtones and snooze, saved countdown presets, and a stopwatch with lap times.

To-Do & Task Manager

Due dates, priorities, subtasks, drag-and-drop reordering, and completion filters.

Calendar

Monthly view with event indicators, a daily agenda timeline, and quick search.

Quick Notes

Card-based notes with checklists, color labels, drag reordering, and fast search.

Currency & Calculator

Currency conversion with offline rate caching, plus a built-in calculator with history.

World Clock

City timezones with relative time-difference badges and drag-and-drop reordering.

Dropbox Sync & Local Backup

Automatic background sync to your private Dropbox account or local .bak file export — zero developer tracking.

Custom Dashboard & Themes

Reorderable, toggleable widgets, color themes with automatic contrast, and a subtle parallax background.
